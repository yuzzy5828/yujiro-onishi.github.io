Yujiro Onishi

I'm a first-year Master student at Intelligent Systems and Informatics Laboratory at The University of Tokyo. My interests is centered on one thing: "How to build intelligent autonomous robots that can think, act, and self-improving by themselves in unseen scinarios?". From the perspective of intelligence, inspired by Charles S. Peirce’s concept of "abduction" and Wittgenstein’s philosophy on logical form, I investigate the essential conditions for intelligence—whether it stems from data-driven approaches, logical semantics, or alternative paradigms. From the perspective of robot learning, I also working on Imitation learing, Reinforcement Learning in robotics. I'm currently an intern at Tokyo Robotics, where I work on perceptive whole-body control of humanoid robots (engaging with the project).
